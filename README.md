## 🔬 What I work on

I'm a software engineering student at **ENIT** (Tunis) building at the intersection of **cybersecurity** and **artificial intelligence**. The question I keep coming back to:

> *How can AI take over the repetitive parts of security work (triage, log analysis, scanning, reporting) while a human stays in control of the decisions that matter?*

- 🤖 **AI agents for security operations**: natural-language and voice interfaces over logs, with approval gates on risky actions
- 🔍 **Security automation**: orchestrating scanning tools and turning raw output into structured findings and reports
- 📈 **Applied machine learning**: fraud detection, sequence models and prediction pipelines
